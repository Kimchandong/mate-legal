Child Safety Standards

Mate(메이트) · Dong

Mate has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any content that sexualizes minors.

Who can use Mate

Mate is only for adults aged 18 and over. Accounts found to belong to minors are removed.

What is prohibited

Sharing, requesting or linking to CSAM; sexual or romantic contact with minors; grooming; sextortion; and any content or behavior that sexualizes, endangers or exploits children.

How to report

In the app, tap a message to report it, and block anyone you do not want to hear from. You can also email dong5084@gmail.com.

What we do

Reports are reviewed by the operator. Violating content is removed, and the accounts involved are permanently banned. Banned-word filtering blocks known harmful terms in messages and nicknames.

We comply with applicable child safety laws and report confirmed CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement.
