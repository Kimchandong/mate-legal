You may not post: abusive, degrading or hateful language; obscene or sexual content; violence or threats; spam or advertising; other people's personal information; anything illegal.

Each user is responsible for the messages they post.

5. Matching

Matching rates and trait analysis are calculated from survey answers for reference only and guarantee no relationship or outcome.

1:1 matching: when you finish every survey of a matching service, a 1:1 chat room opens with the one person whose matching rate with you is the highest, at 90% or more. There is one partner per service; blocking your partner or deleting an account ends the match and its room.

5:5 rooms: some surveys split the people who finished them into two rooms by their score on one trait (at the median of everyone). Your side is set when you first enter and does not change; you only see your side's room.

Matching-rate events: a 1:1 match in the rate band set by the Operator during the event period enters you automatically. Winners are drawn after the event ends (or everyone eligible wins) and are told by notification and in MY.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in MY → Account. This permanently deletes your account, survey records and trait scores, backup, 1:1 matches, chat messages, room memberships and event wins on the server.
