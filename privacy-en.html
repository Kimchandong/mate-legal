Privacy Policy

Mate(메이트) · Dong

Dong (the "Operator") handles the personal information of people who use the Mate app (the "Service") as described below. The Korean version is the original.

1. What we collect

An account ID created automatically (anonymous) when you first open the app.

If you log in with Google or Apple: that account's email address and identifier.

Surveys: which surveys you finished and when, and the trait scores calculated from your answers (position and number of answers per trait). Trait scores are used only to find your 1:1 partner and are never shown to other users.

Backup: your survey answers (the choices you picked), unfinished surveys, notice inbox and app settings are backed up to your account and restored when you log in with the same Google or Apple account after reinstalling or on another device. Only you can see your backup.

1:1 matching: your partner sees only your nickname and the matching rate between you.

Chat: nickname, messages you send, rooms you joined (including your side of 5:5 rooms), reports and blocks.

Events: records of matching-rate event wins.

Notifications: the device token needed for push notifications and your notification settings.

2. Why we use it

Finding your 1:1 partner, checking entry to matching rooms, providing chat, sending notifications, handling reports and preventing abuse, and login and backup so you can continue after reinstalling or on another device.

3. How long we keep it

Chat messages: deleted automatically 30 days after they are sent.

Account and other information: until you delete your account, then deleted immediately.

Reports: a copy of a reported message is kept for 1 year after the report is handled, to deal with the report and prevent repeats.

4. Service providers

Supabase: account and data storage (server).

Expo, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service: delivering push notifications.

Google, Apple: login (if you choose it).

5. Sharing

We do not share personal information with anyone except when required by law. We do not use it for advertising.

6. Your rights

You can delete your account and data in MY → Account.

You can turn notifications off in MY → Notification settings or in your device settings.

Requests to see, correct or delete your data, and questions: dong5084@gmail.com

7. Children

People under 14 may not use the Service.
